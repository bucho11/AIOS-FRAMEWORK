## The agency workflow — where automation fits

```
1. LEAD INTAKE      ← the social agent feeds THIS NODE ONLY
2. CONSULT          ← manual time sink #1
3. SOURCE
4. SCREEN           ← FCRA-governed. Never automate the decision.
5. MATCH            ← manual time sink #2
6. INTERVIEW        ← manual time sink #3
7. TRIAL (always paid)
8. PLACE            ← 15–30% of first-year comp, or flat $2,500–$4,500
9. SUPPORT          ← 30/60/90-day check-ins
10. PAYROLL         ← handed to a specialist. Never reimplement.
```

## Ranked candidates for workflow #2

The operator didn't know what came next (Q14). This is the evidence-based answer:

| Node | Potential | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **2. Consult prep** | **High** | Agent pre-reads the intake form, drafts a needs brief and a realistic local pay range. Saves ~20 min every call and improves the call. **Best first follow-on.** |
| **9. Support check-ins** | **High** | 30/60/90-day check-ins are calendar-driven and templated. Directly protects the replacement guarantee. Easiest win. |
| **6. Interview coordination** | **High, low risk** | Three-party scheduling. Purely mechanical. |
| **5. Match shortlist** | High, bounded | Draft the *fit notes* for a human-picked shortlist. **Never let it pick candidates** — a child's safety rides on that judgement. |
| 3. Sourcing | Medium | Draft outreach yes; evaluate candidates no. |
| 8. Contracts | Medium | Fill a lawyer-approved template. Never generate contract *language*. |
| **4. Screening** | **Do not automate the decision** | FCRA adverse-action rules. Track status, never evaluate results. |
| **10. Payroll** | **Do not build** | Solved by providers. Household employer tax is a compliance minefield. |

## Hard content rule — worker classification

Nannies are **W-2 household employees, not 1099 contractors**, in essentially all
standard placements. **Any content implying 1099 treatment is a real liability
for the client.** This goes in the skill's banned-claims list.

## What actually generates leads

| Format | Job | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| **Reels** | Reach and discovery. Face-to-camera authority. | 2–3/week |
| **Carousels** | Saves and qualification. Checklists, red flags, comparisons. | 1–2/week |
| **Stories** | Nurture, polls, FAQ, urgency on open slots | near-daily |
| Static | Testimonials, open roles, announcements | as needed |

**Cadence:** FB 3–5/week; IG 3–4/week + near-daily Stories. Call it **30–50
posts/month** for vendor sizing.

## Content pillars — the derived answer to Q8

The operator asked for these to be worked out rather than guessed. Derived from
sector research plus the brand's own positioning (trust · love · premium ·
inclusive · 24/7 · customizable). **A hypothesis for the onboarding skill to
confirm with her — not a conclusion.**

| # | Pillar | Serves | Formats | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Trust & vetting** | Families | Reel, carousel | "How we screen every nanny — all 7 steps" |
| 2 | **Parent questions answered** | Families | Reel, carousel | "Nanny vs. babysitter vs. au pair — which do you need?" |
| 3 | **Service fit & availability** | Families | Static, Story | "Now serving [market]" · "2 slots open this month" |
| 4 | **Caregiver spotlights** | **Both sides** | Reel, static | "Meet Maria — 8 years, CPR certified, loves messy art" |
| 5 | **Nanny recruitment** | Nannies | Reel, static | "What we pay, and why we pay it" |
| 6 | **Local presence** | Families | Story, static | Community events, local partnerships |
| 7 | **Parent proof** | Families | Static, Reel | Testimonials, placement stories |

**Pillars 4 and 5 are the ones most agencies miss.** A nanny agency is a
**two-sided marketplace** — content that only speaks to families starves the
supply side, and an agency with no nannies cannot place anyone.

## CTAs that convert for local services

**DMs and calls are the conversion event — not link clicks.**

- "DM **NANNY** for current availability" — best qualifier
- "Comment **CHECKLIST**" — lead magnet trigger
- "Book a free family call" — high intent
- "Call or text us today" — strong on Facebook

## Local signals

City + neighbourhood in bio, captions and on-screen text · location tag on every
post · **small local hashtags** (`#RenoNanny`) not large generic ones
(`#childcare`) · correct category "Child Care Service" · Facebook reviews ·
"Send Message" as the primary CTA button.

## The measurable handoff

The agent's success metric is **qualified intake submissions and DMs**, not
impressions. Wire the intake form's source field back to the post that produced
it, or the whole thing optimises blind.

# Pillars, cadence, and formats — the sector defaults

Derived from 2026 research on local childcare and nanny agencies, plus the brand
positioning captured at onboarding. **Defaults, not laws** — the owner's capacity and
`What works (current)` always win.

## Seven topics (rename in her words during onboarding)

| # | Topic | Audience | Best formats | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Trust & vetting** | families | Reel, carousel | "How we screen every nanny — all 7 steps" |
| 2 | **Parent questions answered** | families | Reel, carousel | "Nanny vs. babysitter vs. au pair — which do you need?" |
| 3 | **Fit & availability** | families | photo, story | "Now serving [market]" · "Two openings this month" |
| 4 | **Caregiver spotlights** | **both** | Reel, photo | "Meet Maria — 8 years, CPR certified, loves messy art" |
| 5 | **Caregiver recruiting** | caregivers | Reel, photo | "What we pay, and why we pay it" |
| 6 | **Local presence** | families | story, photo | community events, partnerships, neighbourhood |
| 7 | **Parent proof** | families | photo, Reel | testimonials, placement stories (with disclosure rules) |

Topics 4 and 5 are the ones agencies forget. A nanny agency is a two-sided
marketplace; content that only speaks to families starves the supply side.

## Cadence

- Instagram: 3–4 posts/week + near-daily Stories
- Facebook: 3–5 posts/week
- Practical default at capacity 4: **2 Reels + 1 carousel/graphic + 1 photo**,
  cross-posted, plus Stories from the same material.
- Reels drive discovery; carousels drive saves and qualification; Stories nurture;
  static is weakest for reach — use it for announcements and proof.

## What the algorithms reward (2026)

Watch time on video; **saves, shares, and DMs** as the strong downstream signals.
Plan content people finish, save, forward, and message about — not content that
only earns likes.

## Calls to action that convert for local services

DMs and calls are the conversion event, not link clicks.
- "DM **CARE** for current availability" — best qualifier (pairs with a
  comment-to-DM automation)
- "Comment **CHECKLIST**" — lead-magnet trigger
- "Book a free family call" — high intent
- "Call or text us today" — strong on Facebook

## Local signals

Market/neighbourhood in the first line and on-screen text; location tag on every
post; **3–5 small local hashtags** (`#RenoNanny`), never large generic ones;
correct business category ("Child Care Service"); Facebook reviews.

## Best-time fallback (when no performance data yet)

Weekday mornings 8–10 and evenings 7–9 in her timezone; Sunday evening for
planning-type content. Replace with the vendor's best-time data as soon as
`learn` has two weeks of posts.

## Filming specs for the owner (plain English)

- Vertical, phone is fine, good light, face to camera when possible
- **60 seconds or less** so the same Reel works on Instagram and Facebook
- One idea per video; say the hook in the first 3 seconds
- No children's faces unless a release is on file

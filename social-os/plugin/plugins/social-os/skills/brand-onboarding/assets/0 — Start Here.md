# Start here

Hi {{owner_first_name}} — this folder is your social media assistant's memory. You
don't have to manage it. You just talk to Claude in Cowork.

## The three things you'll say

- **"Plan my week"** — it plans next week's posts and tells you if it needs a photo
  or a short video from you.
- **"Show me the drafts"** — it shows you what's waiting.
- **"{{approval_word}} [post name]"** — that's the only way anything gets published.
  Nothing goes live without that word from you.

## What's in here

- **1 — Brain / Told to us** — what you've told it about your business and how you
  sound. **Edit these any time.** It reads them before every post.
- **1 — Brain / Learned by us** — what it has learned from your results. You read,
  it writes.
- **2 — Brand Assets** — logos, photos, and photo releases from families.
- **3 — Social** — posts move from *Drafts* → *Approved* → *Published*. If it's in
  Drafts, it has not been posted.
- **3 — Social/Results** — a short report every week on what worked.

## Two promises it keeps

1. It will never post a child's face unless there's a signed release in
   **2 — Brand Assets / Photo releases**. That protects your account and your families.
2. It will never claim you're "licensed", "certified", or "guaranteed" anything
   unless you've written that fact into **What we offer**.

If something ever looks wrong, just tell it in plain words. It remembers your
corrections.

# Start here

Hi {{owner_first_name}} — this folder is your assistant's memory. You don't have to
manage it. You just talk to Claude.

## The three things you'll say

- **"Plan my week"** — it plans next week's posts and tells you if it needs a photo
  or a short video from you.
- **"Show me the drafts"** — it shows you what's waiting.
- **"{{approval_word}} [post name]"** — that's the only way anything gets published.
  Nothing goes live without that word from you.

## The one rule that matters

**Tell it what you want changed. Don't edit the folder yourself.**

Not because you're not allowed — it's your folder. Because of what happens if you
do. Say you open **Brand voice** and take out the exclamation points. Claude doesn't
know you did that. Next time you mention it, Claude writes the change *somewhere
else* — and now there are two documents about how you sound, quietly disagreeing.
Six months later something reads wrong and nobody can work out why.

So: just say it.

| Instead of opening a file | Just say |
|---|---|
| Editing **Brand voice** | "Stop using exclamation points." |
| Editing **What we offer** | "We charge $30 an hour now." |
| Editing **Rules for the AI** | "You can post the availability ones without asking me." |
| Editing **Who we talk to** | "We're going after more overnight families." |
| Adding a note somewhere | "I don't like how these are starting — make them shorter." |

Claude finds the right document, changes it, and fixes anything that was already
built from the old version. **One place, changed properly, every time.**

If you ever do edit something by hand, that's fine — just mention it. Claude checks
for hand edits before it writes, and it'll ask you rather than write over you.

## What's in here

- **0 — Map** — Claude's index of everything. It reads this first, every time.
- **1 — Brain / Told to us** — your rules. How you sound, what you sell, who you're
  talking to, what Claude may do on its own. **These are the only documents that
  change how it behaves.**
- **1 — Brain / Learned by us** — what it's learned from your actual results. It
  writes, you read. This never overrides your rules — if the numbers suggest
  changing one, Claude asks you first.
- **2 — Brand Assets** — logos, photos, and photo releases from families.
- **3 — Social** — posts move from *Drafts* → *Approved* → *Published*. If it's in
  Drafts, it has not been posted.
- **3 — Social / Results** — a short report every week on what worked.

## When something looks wrong

Say so, in whatever words come out. "Why did you do that?" "That's not right." "I
already told you this." All of those work.

Claude will stop what it's doing, find the document that caused it, and fix *that*
— not just the one post. Then it'll tell you in a line or two what changed. If it
needs to know something, it'll ask you one short question, not five.

**"I already told you" is the most useful thing you can say.** It means a fix didn't
stick last time, and Claude goes looking for why.

## Two promises it keeps

1. It will never post a child's face unless there's a signed release in
   **2 — Brand Assets / Photo releases**. That protects your account and your families.
2. It will never claim you're "licensed", "certified", or "guaranteed" anything
   unless you've written that fact into **What we offer**.

## Two audiences, not one

This is the single most important structural fact about their content:

| Audience | What they want | Content that serves them |
|---|---|---|
| **Families** (demand) | Trust, safety, fit, speed | Vetting explainers, testimonials, FAQ, availability |
| **Nannies** (supply) | Good placements, fair pay, respect | Open roles, caregiver spotlights, pay transparency, culture |

A nanny agency is a **two-sided marketplace**. Content that only speaks to
families starves the supply side, and an agency with no nannies cannot place
anyone. The content plan must allocate to both.
